## Glossary

Each entry is one or two sentences. Where a word has a chapter of its own, the entry says where.

### At PRA

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Center | Palm River Academy is an English language center, licensed as one. In writing it is "the center", "PRA" or "Palm River Academy", never a school; in Vietnamese "trung tâm". <!-- allow --> |
| Academic year | August to June, the year that fees, reports and the roster run by. 2026-2027 runs from 4 August 2026 to 4 June 2027. |
| Quarter | One of the four parts of the academic year, 45 class days each. Fees and progress reports run by quarter; it is never called a term. Dates in [The year at PRA](#/handbook/the-year). |
| Stage | Early Years (Nursery and Kindergarten), Primary (Years 1 to 6), Lower Secondary (from Year 7) and Upper Secondary (the teenagers' program). In 2026-2027 Year 9 students are billed as Upper Secondary. |
| Year group | The class level a student is in, set by birthday with a 1 September cut-off. The Current also calls it the level; a class group is a class that combines year groups. |
| Homeroom | A teacher's own class. The homeroom teacher writes the homeroom comment and rates the learner skills on each report. |
| Regular program | The year-long program, as against the short-term Global Program. |
| Global Program | The short-term program, from 3 weeks to 3 months, full day or part day (part day only for Primary and Secondary). No admission fee; the price is given on request. |
| Upper Secondary pathways | Hybrid and Independent, the two Upper Secondary programs, each with a flat yearly fee (150M and 80M VND in 2026-2027) plus the Acellus fee. |
| Acellus | The US-aligned online curriculum that Upper Secondary students follow. PRA pays it for the family (110M VND a year in 2026-2027) and is paid back in cash; no discount ever applies to it. |
| Master Minds | A specialist learning area in Year 1, taught by Seth since 2 October 2026. Two words. |
| Trial day | A day of class before enrolling: 1,600,000 VND, meals included, up to three days, taken off tuition if the child enrolls. |
| ECAs | Extra-curricular activities after class, 15:30 to 16:10, in blocks of 5 to 8 weeks; some free, some with a small fee. |
| Showcase | An event each quarter when the classes show families what they have learned (Quarter 1 on 15 October 2026, Quarter 2 on 17 December 2026). |

### Students and families

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Enrolled, Pending, Past | The three student statuses (active, pending and inactive in the database). A status changes only by hand. |
| Pending | A new or returning student who has not started. Can be invoiced, but is not on the register, in reports or in the counts until set to Enrolled. |
| Past | A student who has left. The record and its history stay; set a leaver to Past rather than deleting them. |
| S number | The student ID (S0001, S0002 and so on), given by The Current to every new student. The student's Google address is the same code in lower case at pra.edu.vn. |
| BLE code | An older kind of student ID, kept by the students who have one because it is printed on invoices already sent. The even older PAL codes became S numbers. |
| Legal first name | The given name printed on progress reports instead of the nickname. For a Vietnamese name it is the last two words of the full name. |
| Partial-day | A student who arrives after the morning lessons, at 11:35. Their report has no academic areas, and the register does not mark them present before they arrive. |
| A(T) | Absent (Traveling): a class day inside a trip recorded on the register. It counts as an absence. |
| Expected end date | The last day a student is expected to come. After it the register stops expecting them and later invoices leave them out; the status still changes only by hand. |
| Family | The parents or guardians of one or more students. Invoices are addressed to the family, and every child of a family comes onto an invoice together. |
| Lead | A family who has asked about joining, before they enroll: one row per family on the Leads tab, with a stage, an owner, a next step and a follow-up date. See [Students, families and enrollment](#/handbook/students). |
| Enrollment form | The five-step form at https://pra.edu.vn/admissions/enroll/. It saves into the Enrollments page of The Current and adds the child as a pending student. It replaced Jotform on 30 September 2026. |
| New-student checklist | The steps for each new student, in the student window: ID, year group, dates, family and parent email, lead, first invoice, form on file, and three ticks a person makes (supply check, mailing lists, no-photo list). |
| Supply check | The checklist tick that saves a Gmail draft about a new student to the year group's teachers and Hien, and adds a To-Do task for Hien. |
| Meals at PRA | Yes, No or not answered, on each student. It decides the parents_menu@ list and whether meals start ticked on a new invoice. |

### Fees and money

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fee announcement | An invoice, as its English heading calls it. |
| Plan | How tuition is paid: early bird, standard, by quarter, installments, weekly, trial day, staff child, or no tuition. |
| Early bird | The lower full-year price, for paying the whole year before the early-bird deadline (20 May 2026 for 2026-2027). Standard is the full-year price paid later (by 30 June 2026). |
| Legacy student | A returning student who keeps last year's early-bird price (one flat amount, 150M VND in 2026-2027). Ticked on the student record. |
| Reduced Quarter 4 | Quarter 4 costs less than the other quarters for the students on the enrolled roster when the 2026-2027 fees were published. A new or returning student pays a full quarter: the **Full-price Quarter 4** box. |
| Sibling discount | 5% off the cheapest child's tuition when two or more children with tuition are on one invoice. Children are listed priciest first so it lands on the cheapest. |
| Admission fee | A one-time fee of 10,000,000 VND for a new student, added by the **New student** box. None for the Global Program. |
| Prorated | A quarter charged only for the class days attended: the quarter price times the days, divided by 45, rounded to 1,000 VND. |
| Invoice number | PRA-2627-0001 onwards. The middle part is the academic year, and the numbers start again at 0001 in a new year. |
| Phiếu thu | The bilingual cash receipt printed for a payment, numbered PT-2627-0001 onwards. |
| Proof of payment | A screenshot or PDF of a bank transfer attached to a payment. Private, seen only by office accounts, never printed on the receipt. |
| Void | A cancelled invoice. It stays in the list and is left out of every total. |

### Progress reports

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Progress report | The one-page report for each student each quarter ("Learning Progress Report" on the page), in English or in English and Vietnamese. See [Progress reports](#/handbook/reports). |
| Learning area | A subject or area on a progress report: English, Cooking, Wellbeing and so on. |
| Tiers | The three kinds of learning area. Academic (Mathematics, Science, English): level, review score, comment and course description. Specialist: level and comment. Vocational: level and a shared course description. |
| The five levels | Emerging, Practicing, On Target, Confident, Moving Beyond, in that order; On Target is the goal. Stored as the values 1, 2, 3, 5, 4, which are labels and not ranks: never renumber them. |
| Progress review, review score | PRA's own quarterly assessment in an academic area, and its score on the report (Years 4 to 6 and Lower Secondary). "Reviews" always means these, never the practice packets. |
| Class reference | The year group's average review score, printed beside each student's score. The head fills it in with the **Class references** button. |
| Summative | The end-of-year assessment; its score appears on the last quarter's report. |
| Course description | One short text per year group and learning area each quarter, saying what was taught. It prints on every report of that year group. |
| Learner skills | The rated skills under "How I learn" on every report, filled in by the homeroom teacher. Early Years has its own set. |
| Ready to review, Published | A teacher sets a finished report to Ready to review; the head publishes it. Only the head can change a published report. |

### The Current and the database

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The Current | PRA's admin app at https://current.pra.edu.vn: students, attendance, invoices, reports, leads, tasks, photos, the newsletter and this handbook. See [The Current, tab by tab](#/handbook/the-current). |
| Office account | A login with the role Super Admin, Head (labelled Head Teacher) or Admin. Teachers have the Teacher role and see less. |
| Supabase | The online service that holds The Current's database, its logins and its private files, on a free plan under admin@palmriveracademy.edu.vn. |
| Table | One list in the database, such as the students or the invoices. Every table of The Current starts with `adm_`. |
| Bucket | A private folder of files in Supabase: student photos, proof of payment, enrollment documents, event photos under review, task files. The app shows a file through a link that lasts an hour. |
| RLS | Row level security: the database's own rules on who may read or change each row. It is why a teacher cannot open invoices even by typing the address. |
| SQL update file | A file in The Current's `supabase` folder, named `updates-` with the date and the topic, that changes the database. Claude writes it; you paste it into Supabase's SQL Editor and press Run. Every file is safe to run twice. |
| Edge function | A small program that runs inside Supabase. The Current has one, which posts to PRA's Facebook Page and holds the Page's key. |
| Offline mode | The Current running without the database, with test data kept in one browser and an "Offline mode" band at the top. Seen on the live site, it means the app cannot reach the database. |
| Secret key | The database key that can do anything. You type it when a script asks; it is never saved in the code and never typed by Claude. |
| `.env` | The settings file beside The Current's code on the laptop: the database address, its public key, the Gmail settings. Never published. |
