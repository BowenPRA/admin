This chapter covers the daily register in The Current, the students it treats differently (partial-day, starting, leaving and traveling), the attendance summary and its printouts, and the Schedule page. Open it when you take or check a register, when a family says a child will be away, when the attendance numbers look wrong, or when the timetable changes.

## Who takes the register

- Teachers take the register for the year groups on their row of the Teachers page (the year groups they teach a learning area in, or are homeroom for). Their homeroom year groups come first.
- Office accounts can take every year group, and fill in any register a teacher missed.
- The register is taken each class day. **Attendance today** on the Home page shows which classes are done, in progress or not taken yet.
- A teacher who sees "You are not linked to any year group yet" needs their year groups ticked on the Teachers page (see [The Current, tab by tab](#/handbook/the-current)).
- Under each register, "Taken by" names who made the marks.

| Task | Office accounts | Teachers |
|---|---|---|
| Take, change and clear marks | Every year group | Their own year groups |
| Add or change a trip | Any student | Students whose register they take |
| Read the summary and print it | Every year group | Their own year groups |
| Change a start date, end date or partial-day time | Yes, in the student window | No (Students is read-only for them) |
| Edit the Schedule | Yes | No, read only |

## Taking the register

1. **Attendance** → **Take attendance**.
2. Pick the day: the arrows move a day at a time (they skip weekends), the date opens a calendar, **Today** comes back to today. A weekend day shows "Weekend: there is usually no attendance to take."
3. Tap a year group. Each chip shows how many are marked ("12/15") or a tick when all are. The last year group you used is remembered on that device.
4. Tap **Present**, **Late** or **Absent** for each student. Each tap saves at once.
5. Or mark the ones who are away, then press **Mark everyone else present**. It reads the day again first, so marks made on another phone in the meantime stay.
6. The speech bubble beside a student opens a **Note**. It saves when you leave the box. A note on its own does not mark anyone.

- Only Enrolled students of that year group are on the register. Pending students are not, until their status is set to Enrolled.
- The counts at the top show Present, Late, Absent, A(T) and **Not marked**, with a coloured bar.

### The marks

| Mark (screen) | Meaning | In the attendance rate |
|---|---|---|
| Present | In class | Counts as attended |
| Late | Came late | Counts as attended |
| Absent | Did not come | Counts as an absence |
| A(T), Absent (Traveling) | A class day inside a recorded trip; shown without anyone tapping it | Counts as an absence |
| Not marked | No mark yet | Not counted; flagged as missing on the printout |

- There is no "Excused". It was removed on 22 September 2026 and the old excused marks became Absent. Bowen's rule: an absence is an absence.
- In Vietnamese the traveling mark reads V(X), "Vắng (đi xa)". The Vietnamese labels for travel have not been read by staff.
- One mark per student per day.

### Changing or clearing a mark

- Tap another button to change a mark.
- Tap the lit button again to clear it ("Tapped the wrong one? Tap the coloured button again to clear it."). Teachers can clear marks too, since 17 September 2026.
- Marks made on another device appear when you come back to the tab.

### On a phone

- Each student has three big buttons on their own line under the name, and the speech bubble beside it.
- A bar along the bottom of the screen keeps the count and **Mark everyone else present** under your thumb. When a year group is complete the bar offers **Next:** and the next year group, and at the end "All n marked".

## Checking the registers

The app does not chase a missing register by itself. To check:

1. Each class day, open Home and look at **Attendance today**. A row reading **Not taken yet** or **In progress** is a register still open.
2. Ask the teacher, or open **Attendance**, pick that year group and fill it in.
3. At the end of a month, open **Attendance** → **Summary** → **Print summary** for the month. The "Class days" figure warns how many marks are missing; each register shows where.
4. Fill in the missing days on **Take attendance** (pick the date), and look at the list of students below 90%.

> **Ask Claude:** "The attendance summary for Year 3 in September looks wrong. Compare it with the marks in the database and tell me which days or students explain the difference, without changing anything."

## Partial-day students

A partial-day student comes only in the afternoon. In Quarter 1 of 2026-2027 there were two afternoon-only students, arriving at 11:35.

- It is set in the student window: **Enrollment** → **Partial day: joins after the morning lessons**, and **Arrives at** (11:35 by default).
- The register shows a small "from 11:35" tag beside their name.
- The mornings are the academic lessons, so their progress reports have no academic areas (Mathematics, Science, English).
- **Mark everyone else present** does not mark them on today's date before their arrival time, and says so: "(name) arrives at 11:35, so is not marked yet. Mark them when they arrive." After that time, or on an earlier date, the button includes them.
- Invoices do not treat partial days differently.

> **Rule:** a partial-day student is not expected before their arrival time. Mark them when they come in, not with the morning register.

## Start and end dates

Both dates are in the student window under **Enrollment**.

- **Start date:** the summary does not expect a student before it, so the days before do not count as missing. The register itself lists every Enrolled student of the year group, whatever their start date.
- **Expected end date:** after it the register lists the student last, with "Expected end date: (date). Mark only if they came in." They are not counted as unmarked and **Mark everyone else present** leaves them out. They can still be marked if they come. The summary stops expecting them, a trip no longer turns their days into A(T), and the date stays in force after their status is set to Past.
- Neither date changes the status. Set a leaver to Past by hand (see [Students, families and enrollment](#/handbook/students)).

## Traveling students

Families often tell PRA ahead of time that a child will be away. A trip records it once, and every class day inside it shows as A(T) on the register without anyone tapping. Bowen's rule (1 October 2026): traveling students still count as absent in the attendance rate.

### Entering a trip

From the register:

1. Tap the speech bubble beside the student.
2. Tap **Mark as traveling**.
3. Fill in **First day away**, **Last day away**, **Where** (a city or country) and a note if useful.
4. Press **Save trip**.

From the student window: **Travel** → **Add a trip**, with the same boxes.

- **Change the trip** (on the register) or the trip in the student window changes the dates or place. Every day inside it changes at once. **Remove trip** deletes it.
- Office accounts can enter trips for anyone. A teacher can enter one for a student whose register they take. Every trip records who made and changed it.

### How a trip shows

- On the register: a "Traveling:" line with the place and dates under the name, and the Absent button lit in violet as **A(T)**. **Mark everyone else present** skips them.
- If the student comes in after all, tap **Present** or **Late**. A real mark on a trip day stands.
- Nothing is written into the marks for a trip; A(T) is worked out from the trip each time. Deleting the trip removes the A(T) days.
- On the summary and the printout: a column of its own (A(T); on the printed register a T, or X in Vietnamese).
- On Home: a "Traveling: …" chip with who is away today. A traveling student counts as registered and as absent in **Attendance today**.

## The Home page card

**Attendance today** on Home has one row per year group with its bar, "n/n students" and how many are absent, and a status: **Done**, **In progress** or **Not taken yet**. A homeroom year group is labelled as yours. The card's link and each row open **Attendance**. Office accounts see every year group; teachers see their own. The greeting band at the top also counts the classes still to register.

## The summary

**Attendance** → **Summary** shows the totals for a period.

1. Pick a month, a quarter or the academic year. The line under it says how many days the period has in the PRA calendar.
2. Pick a year group, or all.
3. Read the cards: one per year group with its attendance rate, the number of students and class days, and the absences (with the A(T) days in brackets). Click a card to show only that year group.
4. Read the table: each student's Present, Late, Absent, A(T), **Days marked** and **Attendance**.

- The rate is present or late out of the days marked. Below 90% shows in amber with a downward arrow.
- Students who have left still show in the periods they were marked in.
- A class day is a weekday inside a quarter on which anyone at PRA was marked, plus any other day that has marks. A weekday on which nobody at all was marked counts as no class (a holiday). The breaks between quarters are left out.
- The on-screen summary shows days marked, not the missing marks. The printout shows the missing marks.

## Printing

### The attendance printout

**Attendance** → **Summary** → **Print summary** opens the printout for the same period and year group, in a new tab.

1. Check the period and the year group in the toolbar, and choose **English** or **Tiếng Việt**.
2. Press **Print**. It prints on A4, landscape. To keep a copy, choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog.

What it holds:

- An overview sheet when several year groups are printed: the totals, each year group, the absences day by day, and the students below 90%.
- Then one register per year group, with its homeroom teacher from the Schedule. A month shows each day; a quarter or the year shows each month. A dash marks a day with no mark. Days before a student's start date or after their end date are hatched when they have no mark; a mark on such a day still shows and counts.
- The "Class days" figure warns how many marks are missing, and each student's row shows their number not marked.
- The notes from the register, under each year group.

Teachers print their own year groups only.

### Class lists

**Students** → **Class lists** prints one A4 page (portrait) per class: photo, birthday, allergies and health notes, who to call, and for office accounts the emergency contacts and pick-up list. Year groups taught as one class share a page. Each page is marked confidential.

### The timetable

**Schedule** → **Print**.

## The schedule

**Teaching** → **Schedule** is the 2026-2027 timetable.

### What it shows

- **Classes**: pick a class to see its week, with its homeroom teacher and room.
- **Teachers**: each teacher's week.
- **Duty**: the playground and gate duty roster.
- A "now" marker follows the clock on today's column.

Everyone can read it; office accounts can change it.

### What it feeds

- Progress reports take the homeroom teacher's name from the class a year group belongs to on the Schedule.
- The attendance printout prints the same homeroom teacher on each register.
- Home takes **Today's lessons** from it, for anyone who is on it.
- The Teachers page compares it with the Teachers rows: **Add n homerooms from the schedule** offers to give a homeroom teacher the right to write those homeroom parts, and "Who teaches what" flags the gaps.

> **Careful:** if the homeroom teacher of a class changes, change it on the Schedule as well as on the Teachers page, or the next progress reports print the old name.

### Editing it

1. **Schedule** → **Edit**.
2. Change what is needed: **Add class**, the class details (name, year groups, homeroom teacher, room), or the rows of a class. A row is either one lesson per day or the same all week (routine, homeroom, break, lunch). Rows can be added, edited, moved and removed.
3. Press **Save**.

- **Built-in schedule** puts back the timetable that ships with the app, after a confirm, and replaces every edit made on this page.

### Where the timetable lives

There are two copies:

- The **built-in** timetable is in the code, in `src/data/schedule.js` in `C:/Users/bowen/admin`. It was made from the "Schedule 2026-2027" spreadsheet. Claude edits it, and a deploy makes it live.
- The **saved** copy is in the database. It exists only once someone has pressed **Save** on the Schedule page, and from then on it is the one everyone sees.

Whether a saved copy exists on the live database has not been checked since 15 October 2026. If one does, a change to the built-in timetable shows only after **Built-in schedule** and **Save** are pressed.

> **Ask Claude:** "From Monday, Year 5's Science lesson moves to Thursday afternoon. Update the built-in schedule in The Current, deploy, and tell me whether I then need to press Built-in schedule and Save on the Schedule page."

A new academic year needs a new timetable, and the Teachers page buttons read the 2026-2027 staff list. Attendance keeps every earlier year's marks, and the summary's list of months and quarters follows the calendar saved in Settings. See [The year at PRA](#/handbook/the-year).

## What can go wrong

- **A start date in the future.** An Enrolled student whose start date is still to come is on the register, but the summary does not expect them before that date, so missing marks are not flagged. A wrong date (day and month swapped, a typo) hides missing marks. Students shows a "starts later" warning on the row; check the date.
- **A Pending student who has started.** They are not on the register and cannot be marked. Set the status to Enrolled on their first day.
- **A day nobody took any register.** The summary treats it as a day with no class, not as missing marks. If every register was forgotten on a class day, mark it afterwards: pick that day on **Take attendance**.
- **Missing marks.** The printout's "Class days" figure and the "Not marked" column show them. Office accounts can fill them in for any year group.
- **A teacher cannot see a year group.** Their Teachers row does not have that year group ticked, or the row is not active.
- **Deleting a student.** It deletes every attendance mark and trip they ever had, with no undo. Set them to Past instead (see [Students, families and enrollment](#/handbook/students)).
- **A trip with the wrong dates.** Every day inside it reads A(T). Correct the trip; the days follow.
- **Two people on one register.** Each tap saves the mark for that student; the last tap wins. **Mark everyone else present** re-reads the day before it marks.
